Where it started

I began my career in business operations and data analysis at SPS Global, a top-100 global BPO in Ho Chi Minh City. Working across 300K+ operational data points, I learned that the best decisions aren't made by instinct — they're built from well-organized, well-interpreted data.

That experience taught me how to lead cross-functional teams, build the first sustainability reporting program from scratch, and communicate complex findings to non-technical stakeholders. I was honored with Best Employee of the Year for that work.

Growing through marketing & demand generation

At SunBytes, a fast-growing B2B tech startup, I stepped fully into digital marketing and growth strategy. I built a three-year demand generation plan from scratch — using cluster analysis and firmographic segmentation to target SMEs in the US and the Netherlands.

The results were immediate: 14× reduction in Cost Per Lead, 100% MQL growth, and 300% increase in web traffic. I learned how to stitch together analytics from 500K+ data points into dashboards that told a story leadership could act on.

Strategy at scale — Mattel

My MBA at the University of Wisconsin-Madison opened the door to Mattel's American Girl brand. As a Marketing Strategy Intern, I worked on the kind of analysis I'd always wanted to do: P&L hypothesis testing, competitor pricing models, and consumer demand netnography.

I built a pricing optimization strategy projected to generate $14MM in incremental revenue, and a resale strategy valued at $800K — all grounded in data, validated by financial modeling.

Into fintech & product analytics

At Capital City Bank, I work at the intersection of banking technology and customer strategy. Analyzing 10M+ customer records, I build CLV models, run behavioral segmentation, and use neural networks on 40M+ online banking transactions to inform product decisions.

This is the work I was built for: turning raw transaction data into product features that improve customer engagement, reduce churn, and grow revenue — in an industry where precision matters.

Where I'm going

I'm pursuing a Master of Science in Data Science & AI at Campbellsville University, completing in 2027, to deepen my expertise in predictive modeling and AI-driven product strategy.

I'm actively targeting Product Strategy, Business Manager, and Analytics roles at Capital One, Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and leading US banks — anywhere that values analytical rigor, strategic thinking, and a relentless focus on the customer.
