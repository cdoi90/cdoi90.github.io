Where it started

I began my career in marketing at Concentrix, a top-100 global BPO, working across digital campaigns and analytics. It was my first taste of how fast digital moves — and I was hooked.

The through-line

From there I moved through banking at Citi, business analysis at SPS Global and growth marketing at SunBytes, rebuilding the website, running marketing campaigns and cutting Cost Per Lead by 14×. Different industries, different titles. Every one of them kept pulling me back to the same place: digital products and the people who use them.

Sharpening the business side

I loved the pace of digital, but I wanted stronger business acumen and product judgment behind it. That took me to the University of Wisconsin-Madison for an MBA in Marketing Analytics & Insights — and to Mattel's American Girl brand, where I saw products from a new angle: pricing. I built a pricing optimization strategy projected to generate $14MM in incremental revenue.

Where I am — and where I'm going

At Capital City Bank, I analyze 40M+ digital banking records to build the behavioral segments that informed the launch of Zelle for Business, ClickSwitch, and WealthAccess — lifting digital engagement 8.5% — and identified the churn drivers behind $1MM in incremental deposit revenue. I'm deepening that with an MS in Data Science & AI at Campbellsville University. Digital transformation is in my blood at this point, and I'm genuinely excited about where it's headed.
