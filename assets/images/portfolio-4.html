01 — Background

The challenge

Mattel's American Girl non-doll category had been underperforming relative to its competitors. Wholesale customers and leaders needed a rigorous, data-backed pricing strategy to take back market share and remove customers' confusion on prices without damaging brand perception.

The challenge required understanding not just the data, but the competitive landscape, consumer price sensitivity, and financial viability — bringing together marketing analytics, finance, and consumer research.

  • Assess FY21–22 financial performance across the non-doll category
  • Model competitive pricing and consumer price sensitivity
  • Build a P&L-validated pricing optimization strategy
  • Identify secondary revenue opportunities (resale, rebranding)
02 — What I Did

Approach & Methodology

The work began with a deep SQL analysis of 2M sales and production records to establish baseline performance across FY21–25 for the non-doll category. I used Tableau to visualize trends and surface anomalies in revenue, margin, and volume.

The pricing strategy was built in three layers:

  • Competitive analysis: Mapped competitors' pricing across key SKUs and identified white-space opportunities where American Girl could command a premium
  • Consumer price sensitivity modeling: Analyzed purchase data and elasticity signals to model how consumers respond to price changes at different price points
  • Identify key variables for price Used regression models to find key factors for Mattel price
  • P&L hypothesis testing: Applied statistical hypothesis testing on 2024 line plan to evaluate pricing scenarios against profitability thresholds — validating the $14MM revenue projection with financial rigor
03 — Solutions & Analysis

Strategy & deliverables

The final pricing strategy recommended strategic price adjustments across the 30K-unit non-doll category, validated across three P&L scenarios — conservative, base, and optimistic — each tested for statistical significance.

A secondary resale strategy was developed in parallel, identifying $800K in incremental revenue opportunity through a structured secondhand marketplace approach grounded in netnography findings.

  • Delivered complete pricing optimization strategy with P&L validation across three scenarios
  • Built competitor pricing map across 20+ key SKUs
  • Identified the relationship between price and multiple factors
  • Hypothesis testing new strategy with 2024 line plan
