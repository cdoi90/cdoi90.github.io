01 — Background

The challenge

Capital City Bank needed to understand why customers were leaving — and what products and experiences would make them stay. With millions of customers across digital banking channels, the data existed. The challenge was turning it into actionable strategy.

The bank's digital products were growing, but retention was inconsistent across demographic and behavioral segments. Leadership needed a data-backed roadmap: which customers to prioritize, what signals predicted attrition, and which product features would drive long-term engagement.

  • Identify the key behavioral and demographic drivers of churn
  • Build a CLV model to prioritize retention investment
  • Inform product feature development with transaction-level data
  • Measure and optimize 10+ digital campaigns across channels

02 — What I Did

Approach & Methodology

I began with a comprehensive CLV analysis using SQL across 10M+ customer records — pulling behavioral journeys, demographic data, product usage patterns, and transaction history to identify churn indicators.

Key steps included:

  • Built SQL pipelines to clean, join, and aggregate 10M+ records across multiple banking tables
  • Created PowerBI dashboards surfacing churn risk scores, customer lifetime value tiers, and segment-level engagement trends
  • Applied K-means clustering on 40M+ online banking transactions to identify behavioral segments hidden in transaction patterns
  • Used neural network analysis to model feature usage patterns and predict which new product features would drive the highest engagement lift
  • Presented findings to product and leadership teams, recommending feature prioritization roadmap

The CLV model surfaced three high-risk churn segments that had previously been treated uniformly, each requiring a distinct retention approach. K-means clustering on transaction data revealed usage patterns that predicted engagement drops 60–90 days in advance.

The neural network analysis on 40M+ transaction records identified which digital features were most strongly correlated with long-term retention — directly informing the product roadmap for new feature development.

  • Delivered CLV tier segmentation enabling targeted retention offers
  • Built churn early-warning system using transaction velocity signals
  • Identified top 3 product features with highest predicted engagement lift
