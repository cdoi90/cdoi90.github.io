01 — Background

The product challenge

The digital banking product team wanted to identify growth opportunities by understanding customer behavior and evaluating which emerging financial features could drive higher engagement. The goal was to leverage customer data to prioritize feature launches, define target segments, and build a data-driven adoption strategy for new digital capabilities — including Zelle for Business, ClickSWITCH, and WealthAccess.

With over 40M+ digital banking records spanning online banking activity, transaction behaviors, and core banking transactions, the raw signal existed. What didn't exist was a framework to turn it into product decisions.

The Core Questions

Who are the right customers for new digital banking features — and maximize adoption after launch?

Key Product Questions
  • —Which customers have the highest potential need for each feature?
  • —What behaviors indicate product readiness?
  • —Which customer segments should be prioritized for launch campaigns?
  • —How can we measure whether new features increase digital engagement?
02 — What I Did

Approach & Methodology

I partnered with digital banking stakeholders to run end-to-end product analytics — from raw behavioral data through to a ranked launch audience for each feature.

1. Customer behavior analysis

Analyzed 40M+ digital banking events and transaction records using SQL and machine learning techniques to understand:

  • Digital login frequency
  • Online banking feature usage patterns
  • Transaction activity across digital banking and core banking systems
  • Customer balances, financial behaviors
  • Existing digital product adoption patterns

2. Customer segmentation & target audience identification

Developed behavioral customer segments to identify the users most likely to adopt each new digital feature. For Zelle for Business, I evaluated the business customer opportunity across:

  • Business customers actively using online banking
  • Existing payment behaviors
  • Alternative P2P payment usage patterns
  • Credit transaction volume and frequency
  • Transaction categories and business types
  • Current digital engagement level
  • Existing feature adoption and digital maturity
Data Sources
  • Digital banking activity logs — Q2 Every Valuable Event (Q2EVE)
  • Core banking transaction data — Silver Lake
  • Client & household data — Marquis CRM
  • Core banking transaction data — S&P500 Business data

03 — Solutions & Analysis

Feature opportunity analysis

Zelle for Business

Analyzed customer payment behaviors to identify:

  • Which business customers were already using alternative payment methods
  • Which segments had frequent payment activity
  • Which industries showed the strongest adoption potential
Recommendation

Identified the top 3 business segments to prioritize during product launch, ranked on transaction behavior, engagement level, and feature fit.

ClickSWITCH & WealthAccess

Applied the same segmentation framework across both features to:

  • Identify customers with strong cross-product potential
  • Understand digital maturity levels across the base
  • Recommend high-propensity audiences for feature adoption

The result was a repeatable model — one segmentation framework the product team could apply to any future feature, rather than a one-off analysis tied to a single launch.
