01 — Background

The challenge

SunBytes was a fast-growing B2B tech startup with an ambitious vision — but a fragmented, underperforming demand generation engine. The company needed to build a scalable, data-driven go-to-market strategy to reach SMEs in the US and the Netherlands.

Without structured segmentation, the company was spending too much to acquire leads that didn't convert. The marketing team had data from multiple platforms but lacked a unified view of the funnel or a coherent strategy to act on it.

  • Build a three-year demand generation plan from scratch
  • Define market segments using cluster and firmographic analysis
  • Reduce Cost Per Lead dramatically while improving lead quality
  • Create unified reporting across 500K+ data points from multiple platforms

02 — What I Did

Approach & Methodology

I began with a full digital ecosystem audit — mapping every channel, understanding the current data infrastructure, and identifying the biggest efficiency gaps in the funnel.

The strategy was built in parallel tracks:

  • Segmentation: Applied cluster analysis and firmographic analysis to define SME segments across the US and Netherlands — identifying the highest-value target personas
  • Campaign optimization: Ran 50+ A/B tests across Google Ads, email, and SEO to identify top-performing messaging and creatives. Achieved AdWords Quality Score of 8/10
  • SEO implementation: Used Ahrefs and SEMRush to analyze search intent, implement the company's first SEO strategy, and boost organic traffic by 300%
  • Unified reporting: Built a Looker dashboard integrating 500K+ data points from all channels into a single lead generation funnel view — reducing reporting time by 80%
  • Led a 7-member cross-functional team to execute the channel revamp
